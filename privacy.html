Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains how Dial for Help handles information submitted through this website.

Information we collect

When you submit a service request or contact form, we may collect your name, phone number, email address (if provided), city or locality, request details and messages. Customer accounts may also store account credentials in protected form and account-related request records.

How we use information

  • Review and coordinate your request with relevant service providers.
  • Check availability, price, timing and other booking details and communicate them to you.
  • Respond to contact messages, maintain request tracking and chat history, protect the website and prevent abuse.

Sharing

We do not publish customer contact details. We may share information needed to arrange a requested service with relevant providers, hosting, database, email, authentication and security vendors, or when legally required. Provider contacts and internal notes are intended for authorized administrators only.

Retention and security

We retain records as needed to operate, support, secure and resolve requests and comply with applicable requirements. We use technical safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Accounts and third-party sign-in

If Google or Facebook sign-in is enabled, the sign-in provider may share profile details such as name, email and provider account identifier. Their own privacy policies also apply. Social sign-in availability may vary.

Your choices

You can contact us to ask about your information, request correction or deletion where applicable, or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your request before responding.
